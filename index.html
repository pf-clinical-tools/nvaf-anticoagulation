Anticoagulation Risk Assessment Tool in NVAF

CHA₂DS₂-VASc + ORBIT with frailty-informed net clinical benefit. For clinicians only.

1. Inputs

Tick all that apply. Outputs update automatically.

CHA₂DS₂-VASc – Stroke risk (no OAC)
CHA₂DS₂-VASc: 0 Stroke risk: ≈0.2–0.9% / year

Men: consider OAC from CHA₂DS₂-VASc ≥2; women: from ≥3. In frailty, even “moderate” stroke risk may be highly disabling.

ORBIT – Major bleeding risk (on OAC)
Age ≥75 is derived automatically from CHA₂DS₂-VASc to ensure internal consistency.
ORBIT: 0 Bleeding risk: ≈2.5% / year (low)

Use ORBIT to highlight and modify bleeding risk; do not use it alone to deny OAC where stroke risk is high.

2. Outputs & Interpretation

Approximate annual risks and a qualitative statement about net clinical benefit in a frail older adult.

Stroke risk (no OAC)

≈0.2–0.9% / year
CHA₂DS₂-VASc 0.

Major bleeding risk (on OAC)

≈2.5% / year
ORBIT 0 (low).

Effect of DOAC

Stroke reduction ≈60–70%
In AF trials, DOACs typically reduce stroke by ≈60–70% and intracranial haemorrhage by ≈50% vs warfarin.

Net clinical benefit: set inputs

Tick CHA₂DS₂-VASc and ORBIT factors to generate a brief statement to support shared decision-making.

  • Falls alone rarely offset the stroke-prevention benefit of anticoagulation.
  • Reassess as frailty, renal function and bleeding history change.
